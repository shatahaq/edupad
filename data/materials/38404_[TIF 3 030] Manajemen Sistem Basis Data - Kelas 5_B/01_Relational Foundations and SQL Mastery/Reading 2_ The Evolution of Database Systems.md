## The Evolution of Database Architectures

Understanding database history illuminates why modern relational and hybrid architectures are designed the way they are.

**⏳ Timeline of Database Paradigms:**

1. **1960s - Hierarchical & Network Models:** Tightly coupled data navigation (e.g., IBM IMS, CODASYL), prone to rigid schema lock-in.
2. **1970 - Relational Model Inception:** Edgar F. Codd published his landmark paper decoupling logical relations from physical storage.
3. **1980s–1990s - Commercial RDBMS Era:** Standardization of SQL, growth of Oracle, IBM DB2, PostgreSQL, and MySQL.
4. **2000s - The NoSQL Revolution:** Prioritizing horizontal scalability, BASE semantics, and flexible schema for big data (Key-Value, Document, Graph).
5. **Present - NewSQL & Multi-Model DBs:** Combining relational ACID guarantees with horizontal distributed scaling (e.g., CockroachDB, Spanner).
